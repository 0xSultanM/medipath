Red-flag symptoms — go to the ER

These match the deterministic rules the symptom checker and chatbot use (emergency_alert_rules). If you notice them, stop reading and call.

❤️ Cardiac
  • Crushing / pressure-like chest pain, esp. spreading to arm, jaw or back
  • Chest pain with sweating, nausea or breathlessness
  • Sudden fainting or very irregular heartbeat
🫁 Respiratory
  • Cannot speak full sentences due to breathlessness
  • Blue lips or fingertips, gasping
  • Stridor or choking
🧠 Stroke (FAST)
  • Face droop · Arm weakness · Speech slurring → Time: call immediately
  • Sudden severe headache with vision loss or confusion
🩸 Bleeding / trauma
  • Bleeding that won't stop after 10 min of pressure
  • Vomiting blood or black stools
  • Head injury with loss of consciousness
🐝 Anaphylaxis
  • Swelling of tongue/throat after food or medicine
  • Widespread hives with breathing difficulty
  • If an epinephrine auto-injector exists, use it first — then call.
👶 Pediatric crisis
  • Infant <3 months with fever ≥38°C
  • Child unresponsive, limp, or severely dehydrated (no urine 8h+)
  • Non-fading rash with fever
⚕️ Poisoning / overdose
  • Suspected medicine overdose — bring the packaging to the ER
  • Chemical ingestion: don't induce vomiting unless told to
  • Poison control: +880 1700-000003
📴Offline fallback: this page and its numbers are stored on the device after first load (service worker cache), so they open without network. Bookings and the chatbot are not offered as substitutes during an emergency.
